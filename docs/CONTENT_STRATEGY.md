# AI Daily Diff — content strategy

Approved direction: 28 September 2026. Public channel language is English.

## Channel promise

Discover what you can newly do with AI: what changed, who can use it, where to start
and what the evidence actually supports. Serve everyday users, professionals and builders through distinct paths,
with complete, understandable answers. Each episode names its primary audience;
no episode is expected to appeal to all three audiences. The desired result is repeat visits through
usefulness and trust; no format, metadata or schedule can guarantee viral growth.

## Channel description

Discover what you can newly do with AI. AI Daily Diff covers important updates to
ChatGPT and OpenAI models, Claude, Gemini and leading open models for everyday life,
professional work and building with AI—plus useful
repositories, agents and documented AI architectures.

Short news explains what changed, who can use it and where to start. Practical guides
explore workflows and tools in more depth. We distinguish official demonstrations,
research claims and our own tests, and link the primary sources.

Subscribe for the next AI development worth understanding.

## Audience paths

| Audience key | Playlist | Public description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| everyday | AI for Everyday Life | Useful AI capabilities for everyday tasks, creativity and learning. Clear explanations, accessible first steps and important limits. |
| professional | AI for Work & Productivity | Practical AI developments for professional work: documents, analysis, collaboration and reliable workflows, with access and limitations explained. |
| builder | AI for Developers & Researchers | Models, repositories, agents and documented architectures for developers and researchers. Technical evidence, setup requirements and practical tradeoffs. |

Choose one primary audience per episode: everyday, professional or builder. Assign
its audience path and relevant topic playlist. Add other audience playlists only when
the actual content serves them; viewers should find an appropriate next episode rather
than every video being placed in every path.

An episode may belong to multiple relevant playlists. Daily, Method and Deep are
formats, not substitutes for topical navigation. Defer vendor-specific playlists
until their archives have useful depth. Do not add an unrelated video to fill a playlist.

Each topic has a production queue. Candidate states are candidate, source_verified,
editorial_ready, queued, published, deferred and rejected. Keep the original event date,
primary URLs, audience/task, precise new delta, archive comparison, intended format,
next review date and reasons for deferral/rejection. Queue only after source, coverage,
editorial and technical checks; publication requires the confirmed receipt.

## Coverage and production rules

Every episode dated 2026-09-28 onward requires the coverage_review contract in
prompts/editorial.md: openai, anthropic, google, repositories and architectures.
Sources must be actually read; unavailable evidence must be marked unavailable and
blocks production until review is completed. Checking all scopes never forces publishing
all scopes. Give every new model release found an explicit editorial decision.

Daily contains at most three strong timely stories, including multiple model releases
in the same vertical. Preserve strong overflow, including late arrivals after that day's
episode, for the next eligible slot; recheck freshness and evidence before publishing.
The existing cadence remains one Daily on weekdays and one weekly Method/Deep on
Saturday. A queue is not a promise to publish an unknown future release.

Repositories qualify through a useful AI task and verifiable artifact even without a
flagship connection. Distinguish launch/release changes from old tutorials. Architectures
require primary technical evidence; never infer a closed model's internals from outputs.
Label research-only claims and distinguish model architecture from agent orchestration.

## Packaging and learning

Lead with a recognisable task or capability, name the exact artifact, fulfil the title's
promise and provide a first step. Write unique descriptions with natural search phrases
for the actual topics. Add source links and only verified chapters. Invite viewers to a
relevant next guide or playlist when one actually exists; do not withhold today's answer.

Initial channel audit: empty description, no playlists, two videos, three views and zero
subscribers. This is setup evidence, not a basis for statistical performance conclusions.
Review future impressions, CTR, retention, traffic sources and returning viewers when
available. Compare suitable samples and record uncertainty; do not invent keyword volume
or claim that metadata changes caused growth without supporting evidence.
