# Playlist prompt — AI Architectures & Research Explained

Read prompts/editorial.md, prompts/playlist.md and the chosen format prompt.
Set primary_playlist: architectures and prompt_profile: architectures. primary_audience must be builder.

Use kind: daily for a timely mechanism update or deep for a supported explanation.
A practical Method task belongs in another suitable playlist.

## Focus

One documented model or system architecture: components, data flow, supported consequences and unresolved limits. Cite the original technical paper/report/code.

## Specialization and delivery

State prerequisites. Explain the mechanism with a grounded example and distinguish research claims, released code and usable products. Label agent orchestration separately from model architecture.

Use the builder overlay to adapt a technical Radar lead: specify the technical
question, inputs, components/data flow, changed mechanism and consequential tradeoff.
Read the original paper/report sections supporting those details; distinguish its
reported experiment from independently established results. A schematic or CPU toy
may illustrate the mechanism but cannot reproduce a large model's benchmark quality.
Explain availability of weights/code separately from a paper's publication date.
If the relevant internals are undisclosed, defer the mechanism claim rather than
invent an architecture lesson. A Deep may be a documented technical explanation;
never call its source diagrams "our execution" or invent captured output.
Package the supported mechanism and question in the title, label research in the
opening, and promise further documented explanations instead of breakthrough hype.

Write one viewer question before the title. Every story, slide, example and source
must serve that question and this playlist's description. Research all mandatory
scopes, then defer strong leads outside this brief to their proper queues. Format
and SEO choices must preserve the subject and audience level.

## Exclude

Closed-model internal speculation, benchmark headlines without a mechanism, generic model releases and app integrations.

## When another playlist is appropriate

Use agents for practical agent/tool coordination, models for release capabilities, repositories for setup-focused artifact reviews. Choose one placement before authoring; this guidance never authorizes
secondary memberships. Rework a mismatched draft or leave it pending.

## Approval

Record playlist_review with status approved, reviewer and a specific whole-video
reason. Verify primary_audience, primary_playlist and prompt_profile agree with the
actual video and generated description before queueing. A passing technical check
or one relevant segment does not approve placement.
