# Playlist prompt — How to Use AI — Practical Guides

Read prompts/editorial.md, prompts/playlist.md and the chosen format prompt.
Set primary_playlist: guides and prompt_profile: guides. Choose exactly one primary_audience: everyday, professional or builder.

Use kind: method. A Daily roundup is not a guide; Deep is reserved for another
appropriate playlist.

## Focus

One clear how-to question answered with ordered steps, prerequisites, a useful expected result, recovery and limits. The viewer must be able to begin the named task.

## Specialization and delivery

Choose one audience before drafting. Match instructions to its knowledge, identify the exact model/tool and separate documented directions from our tested results.

Adapt the established Method prompt to one supported task:

- Everyday: app/account prerequisites, a safe everyday input, exact interface actions,
  a sample prompt when useful, what output to expect and how to check or correct it.
- Professional: role, work input, permitted data/tools, steps toward a deliverable,
  human review and recovery. Use a public or invented sample instead of real client data.
- Builder: environment/version, installation/authentication requirements without
  secrets, commands or configuration, expected artifact, verification and troubleshooting.

The Radar's Prompt or Workflow of the Day is a lead, not a proven recipe. Verify
each instruction against the exact current docs. If we ran it, retain the captured
output; if documented only, label that limitation. Do not add a synthetic benchmark
to turn a documented guide into a measured improvement.
Write the title as the selected audience's actual how-to question. Fulfil it in the
video and companion brief; a follow-up may extend the task but cannot contain a
missing essential step from the promised guide.

Write one viewer question before the title. Every story, slide, example and source
must serve that question and this playlist's description. Research all mandatory
scopes, then defer strong leads outside this brief to their proper queues. Format
and SEO choices must preserve the subject and audience level.

## Exclude

Multi-story news roundups, incomplete teasers, unverified setup commands and brief first-step suggestions marketed as a complete tutorial.

## When another playlist is appropriate

Use repositories when evaluating the artifact is central; agents for explaining coordination rather than teaching setup; an audience path for a mixed briefing. Choose one placement before authoring; this guidance never authorizes
secondary memberships. Rework a mismatched draft or leave it pending.

## Approval

Record playlist_review with status approved, reviewer and a specific whole-video
reason. Verify primary_audience, primary_playlist and prompt_profile agree with the
actual video and generated description before queueing. A passing technical check
or one relevant segment does not approve placement.
